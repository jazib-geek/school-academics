# School Management ERP — Portfolio Features

Portfolio copy based on the features present in the application. “School Management ERP” is used as a descriptive project name.

## 1. Short version — projects listing

### School Management ERP

*School operations, academics, and finance in one connected platform.*

A comprehensive school management platform that brings admissions, student records, attendance, fee collection, examinations, accounting, and payroll into one system. Built for schools with multiple campuses, it includes dedicated staff workspaces, coordinator proforma reporting, live campus alerts and chat, an exam-paper builder, detailed management reports, and APIs for parent-app integration.

**Key features:** Multiple-campus management · Student administration · Fees and accounting · Attendance and payroll · Exams and question bank · Teacher and coordinator workspace · Teacher conduct · Real-time campus alerts · Reporting and analytics

**Technology:** React, ASP.NET Core, Microsoft SQL Server.

## 2. Comprehensive version — project detail page

### School Management ERP

*One platform to manage the school day—from admissions and classroom activity to fee collection and staff payroll.*

We developed a comprehensive school management platform that connects administrative, academic, and financial workflows. It gives school administrators, accountants, teachers, and coordinators dedicated tools for their responsibilities, while helping management oversee campus activity through dashboards and detailed reports.

The solution combines a web-based administration system, a mobile-oriented staff workspace, real-time notifications and messaging for campus teams, an academic question bank and paper builder, and APIs that support parent-facing applications.

### Student admissions and records

Maintain a central record of each student and their family throughout their time at school.

- Manage admissions, student profiles, registration details, and class and section assignments.
- Store parent and guardian information, contact details, and family relationships.
- Link siblings through shared family records.
- Transfer students between classes and sections.
- Update student information and fee details individually or in bulk.
- Review attendance, conduct, and activity history from student records.
- Generate admission lists, enrollment summaries, family directories, locality reports, and age and birthday lists.

### Multiple-campus administration

Support school operations across multiple campuses with campus-specific settings and controlled access.

- Access campus dashboards and an authorized overview across campuses.
- Configure campus profiles, branding, classes, sections, and administrative settings.
- Manage campus users and assign permissions for individual features and reports.
- Standardize operational workflows while maintaining campus-specific records.

### Student attendance and absence follow-up

Give staff practical tools to record attendance and investigate recurring absences.

- Mark and review student attendance by class and date.
- Authorize short leave for students when policy allows.
- Track individual attendance histories.
- Manage absence follow-up records, including structured reasons when contact attempts are unsuccessful.
- Review student leave applications.
- Generate daily summaries, attendance registers, absence lists, and reports across selected periods.
- Identify students absent over multiple days for further follow-up.

### Fee management and collections

Bring fee generation, receipt handling, and outstanding balances into a connected workflow.

- Generate tuition fees and additional fund charges for individual students or groups.
- Update student fee settings and manage concessions.
- Record collections and produce printable receipts.
- Review transaction histories and student and family balances.
- Void receipts through permission-controlled workflows.
- Record fee collections as cash or online and reflect payment mode on receipts and reports.
- Process security deposit refunds with an auditable history separate from routine fee receipts.
- Prepare and print fee reminder notices from configurable templates.
- Track unpaid fees, partial payments, and outstanding amounts by class or family.
- Analyze overdue balances, collection trends, recovery rates, expected income, and the effect of concessions.

### Accounting and financial oversight

Support the school’s daily financial administration alongside fee collection.

- Create cash receipt and cash payment vouchers.
- Maintain account settings and review account ledgers.
- Access cash books and account summaries.
- Perform day-closing workflows with a cash-handover preview that separates online fee receipts from physical cash and summarizes tuition and other daily collections.
- Review franchise royalty share calculations where multi-campus agreements apply.
- Edit or remove vouchers when authorized, with permission-controlled access.
- Review income, profit-and-loss, and fee balance reports.
- Monitor collection performance and financial activity over selected periods.

### Examinations, marks, and results

Manage assessment records and turn marks into useful reports for staff and school leadership.

- Set up examinations and enter student marks.
- Record detailed subject assessment components.
- Review marksheets and individual student results.
- Generate individual and batch result cards in multiple layouts.
- Produce award lists, position lists, and academic progress reports.
- Analyze examination outcomes by teacher and subject.
- Use teacher performance views to support academic review.

### Question bank and exam-paper builder

Provide academic teams with a dedicated workspace for preparing examination papers.

- Organize curriculum content by class, subject, and chapter.
- Maintain a reusable question catalog.
- Build papers through manual question selection or automatic selection from chapters.
- Include multiple-choice, short-answer, and long-answer questions.
- Configure paper sections, question order, marks, and optional-question requirements.
- Add mathematical expressions, formatted text, and question images.
- Save and revise papers with autosave support.
- Adjust print layouts and produce branded examination papers.

### Timetables and examination schedules

Keep teaching assignments and academic schedules accessible to the people who use them.

- Assign teachers to classes and subjects.
- Create and edit class timetables.
- Configure timetable structures, periods, and breaks.
- Give teachers access to their own timetables and assignments.
- Prepare, publish, and print examination date sheets.

### Daily diary and classroom updates

Maintain a shared record of daily academic communication.

- Upload and review class diary entries.
- Browse diaries by class and date.
- Provide diary access through staff workspaces.
- Track diary replacement history for accountability.
- Support parent-app access to diary information through APIs.

### Employee management, attendance, and payroll

Connect staff records and attendance with monthly salary preparation.

- Maintain employee profiles and designations.
- View live attendance records and monthly attendance summaries.
- Import and manage employee attendance data.
- Request and approve attendance corrections through a permission-controlled workflow.
- Calculate salaries using attendance, working days, and configured payroll rules.
- Apply late-arrival and absence deductions.
- Manage salary components, allowances, bonuses, loans, advances, and adjustments.
- Recalculate individual employee salaries when needed.
- Produce printable salary summaries and slips.

### Teacher and coordinator workspace

Give employees convenient access to the tools relevant to their responsibilities.

- View personal attendance, teaching assignments, timetables, and salary adjustments.
- Record student attendance and conduct from the mobile-friendly employee portal.
- Access examinations, date sheets, and class diaries.
- Turn individual portal capabilities on or off per employee—attendance, timetables, subject allocation, datesheets, diaries, lesson plans, exam detail, student conduct, and teacher conduct—so each staff member sees only what their role requires.
- Receive real-time portal notifications for campus events that affect their work.
- Give authorized academic staff access to subject allocation and teacher analysis, including timetable and allocation editing from the portal when permitted.
- Complete structured coordinator daily proforma reports in the employee workspace, with draft and submission flows aligned to the school’s inspection checklist.
- Let academic heads monitor submitted coordinator reports, review concerns, and print the multi-sheet proforma for records.

### Teacher conduct

Support professional accountability with conduct notes tied to staff records.

- Define conduct types and tags, including positive, neutral, and corrective categories.
- Record conduct notes for teachers from the employee portal or campus administration, subject to record and view permissions.
- Review per-teacher histories and printable day or date-range reports filtered by staff, polarity, and keywords.
- Notify relevant campus users when new teacher conduct is recorded.

### Student conduct and parent communication

Keep behavioral records and school communication connected to student and family information.

- Record and review student conduct.
- Access student-level conduct histories and reports.
- Manage family login accounts.
- Publish school news, events, and announcements.
- Support parent-app integration for linked children, attendance, fees, results, diaries, and school updates.
- Provide APIs for parent leave requests and conduct acknowledgments.

### Stationery and stock management

Track school supplies from purchase through distribution.

- Maintain a stationery item catalog.
- Record purchases and staff handovers.
- Review available stock.
- Generate item-level movement and expense reports.

### Campus collaboration and alerts

Keep authorized staff aware of operational activity without leaving their workspace.

- Deliver live campus notifications for admissions, fee receipts, diary uploads, student and teacher conduct, leave applications, employee attendance events, attendance change requests, and completed day closings.
- Browse grouped notification history from the campus header.
- Chat with other campus users in real time, with read receipts and online presence.
- Set personal reminders with in-app alarms so follow-ups are not missed.

### Reporting, permissions, and accountability

Help school teams find relevant information while controlling access to sensitive operations.

- Access reports covering students, attendance, fees, examinations, accounts, and inventory.
- Filter reports by relevant classes, dates, sessions, and other criteria.
- View charts and summaries for operational and financial analysis.
- Produce branded, printable reports and documents.
- Control access to modules, actions, and reports through user permissions.
- Review activity logs and receipt-void history.

### Value for school operations

The platform helps schools maintain consistent records, simplify recurring administrative work, and coordinate responsibilities across departments. By connecting attendance, academics, fees, accounting, and payroll, it gives management a clearer view of daily operations and the information needed to follow up on issues.

### Technology stack

React, Tailwind CSS, ASP.NET Core Web API, Entity Framework Core, Microsoft SQL Server, JWT authentication, Chart.js, and mathematical editing and rendering with MathLive and KaTeX.
